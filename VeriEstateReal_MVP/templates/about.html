PROJECT SCOPE

About this prototype

A demonstration of document processing, rules, machine learning, Flask, and SQL.

WHAT IT DEMONSTRATES

Prototype capabilities

  • Property listing intake and SQLite storage
  • PDF/TXT text extraction; optional image OCR
  • Suggested field extraction
  • Explainable rule-based mismatch checks
  • Scikit-learn duplicate-candidate classifier
  • Precision, recall, F1 and confusion matrix on synthetic data
  • Human review notes and CSV export
LIMITATIONS

What it does not do

  • No government portal integration.
  • No title or ownership authentication.
  • No fraud-free certification.
  • Demo ML metrics use synthetic data only.
  • Image OCR needs a separate Tesseract installation.
  • No login or production security; do not expose it publicly with real personal data.
